Seeya / Accessibility Map - research demo (all data simulated)

Three interactive pages and one PDF. The pages need a computer browser (Chrome, Safari or Edge): double-click, no install, no internet. On a phone, or in an email preview, open Seeya-demo-walkthrough.pdf instead; the pages will look empty there because previews do not run scripts.

index.html   - the passenger's phone: one arriving passenger, Hua (72, from Shanghai, Mandarin, asks for a wheelchair on arrival).
               Press the right-arrow key to move to the next beat. Language selector on the right (English / Francais / Chinese).
               Keys: P = presentation mode (phone only), A = auto-play, M = the same moment on other screens (feature phone, staff, meeting person, airport desk).
               Check "no acceptance" to see what happens when nobody takes the request.
desk.html    - the airport coordination desk (English / Francais). Opens in its own window; if you open index.html in the same browser it follows the phone beat.
airline.html - an airline's one-flight view (English / Francais).

Everything on screen is simulated: people, times, request states, staff positions, counts, the 10-minute window. The base map is traced from Toronto Pearson's public Terminal 1 arrivals map.
